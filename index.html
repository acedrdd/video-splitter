Auto clip

Finds the standout moments in a long video by itself. It listens for natural pauses, builds 20 to 60 second candidates that start and end on a pause, scores them by energy and delivery, and cuts the best ones. No account, no key, nothing leaves your phone.

This picks moments from how the audio sounds (energy, pauses, delivery), not from what is said, so it is a good first guess rather than a mind reader. Cuts are lossless and can start a second or two early because they snap to a keyframe. Works with MP4 and MOV videos.
